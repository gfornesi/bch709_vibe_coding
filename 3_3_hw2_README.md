Do the top 200 most variable yeast stress-response genes in first 30 columns, cluster into distinct expression patterns, and what biological processes characterize each cluster? Describe what each of the 4 clusters represents in terms of stress response (1 sentence per cluster)
There seems to be distinct expression patterns in each of the four clusters. Each cluster is characterized by differential upregulation and downregulation of genes in different stress conditions caused by heat-shocking treatments to different extents.

Cluster 1 (blue):
This cluster of genes is upregulated during short heat shock, downregulated during no heat shock, and upregulated during the slight temperature increased. 
Cluster 2 (purple): 
This cluster of genes has the least distinct expression patterns, but it appears most genes are downregulated during short and long heat shocks but upregulated during the slight temperature increases. 
Cluster 3 (green): 
This cluster of genes is downregulated during short heat shock, upregulated during no heat shock, and downregulated during the slight temperature increases. 
Cluster 4 (pink):
This cluster of genes is upregulated during short and long heat shocks, but downregulated during the slight temperature increases
